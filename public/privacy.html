1. Introduction and Scope

Welcome to Akosil ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are strongly committed to keeping secure any information we obtain from you or about you. This Privacy Policy describes our practices with respect to personal data we collect from or about you when you access Akosil, utilize our document extraction and Optical Character Recognition (OCR) services, interact with our third-party integrations (such as Google Sheets), or otherwise use our platform (collectively, the "Services").

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, including but not limited to the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR). By using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by the data handling practices described in this document.

2. Information We Collect

To provide our Services effectively, we collect several categories of information, which may include personal data. The scope of data collection depends on how you interact with Akosil.

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Data: When you register for an account, we collect your email address, full name, and authentication credentials. If you utilize single sign-on (SSO) providers like Google, we receive basic profile information (such as your name and email) permitted by your configuration with that provider.
  • Billing Information: If you subscribe to a paid tier (Professional or Enterprise), payment processing is handled securely by our third-party payment processor, Paystack. We do not process, store, or transmit full credit card numbers on our servers. We only retain billing history, subscription status, and transactional metadata.
  • Customer Support Data: Information you provide when you contact us for technical support, including crash logs, diagnostic information, and written correspondence.

2.2 User-Generated Content and Document Data

  • Uploaded Documents: When you upload images, PDFs, or other document formats for OCR processing, we temporarily process these files to extract text and tabular data.
  • Zero-Storage Architecture: We employ a strict data-minimization approach. Uploaded documents are processed in-memory for extraction purposes and are promptly discarded after the text is converted into spreadsheet rows. We do not permanently store the raw images of your proprietary documents unless explicitly required by an active, uncompleted background task.
  • Extracted Data: The resulting extracted textual and tabular data (CSV data) is stored securely in our database (Supabase) to allow you to view, edit, and export your workspaces across sessions.

2.3 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage and Telemetry Data: We automatically log standard technical information when you access the Services. This includes IP addresses, browser types, operating systems, referring URLs, device identifiers, and interaction timestamps.
  • Cookies and Tracking Technologies: We use cookies (small text files stored on your device) and similar tracking technologies to maintain session state, remember user preferences, and analyze platform traffic. You may control cookie preferences through your browser settings, though disabling essential cookies may render parts of the application unusable.

3. How We Use Your Information

We only process your personal data when we have a lawful basis to do so under the NDPA and applicable global regulations. We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Service Provision: To authenticate users, provide OCR capabilities, maintain workspace state, and facilitate exports to localized environments or third-party integrations.
  • Platform Improvement: To monitor system performance, troubleshoot technical anomalies, and optimize our proprietary extraction algorithms. We do not use your private, extracted tabular data to train public machine learning models without your explicit opt-in consent.
  • Communication: To send critical transactional emails (e.g., password resets, billing receipts, security alerts) and, subject to your opt-in preferences, promotional or feature-update newsletters.
  • Security and Fraud Prevention: To enforce our Terms of Service, monitor for unauthorized access attempts, mitigate DDoS attacks, and protect the overall integrity of the Akosil infrastructure.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable Nigerian laws, lawful court orders, or governmental requests.

4. Third-Party Integrations and Data Sharing

4.1 Google Workspace and Google Sheets Integration

Akosil offers an integration with Google Sheets, allowing users to seamlessly export and synchronize their extracted tabular data to their personal or organizational Google Drive. By utilizing this feature, you authorize Akosil to access your Google account via OAuth 2.0.

  • Scope of Access: We request only the narrowest possible permissions required to create new spreadsheets and write row data to the spreadsheets you explicitly select or create via the Akosil interface.
  • Data Handling Requirements: Akosil's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Token Storage: OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest within our database. We do not use Google OAuth tokens to access any Google Drive files other than those created by or explicitly connected to the Akosil application.

4.2 Service Providers

We employ trusted third-party service providers to facilitate our Services. These include:

  • Infrastructure and Database Hosting: Supabase and Vercel for secure cloud hosting, database management, and edge routing.
  • Payment Processing: Paystack for secure, PCI-DSS compliant subscription billing and transaction routing.

We execute stringent Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) with these sub-processors, ensuring they adhere to security standards commensurate with or stricter than our own, and they are strictly prohibited from utilizing your personal data for their own independent marketing or commercial purposes.

5. Data Security and Retention

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, destruction, loss, alteration, or misuse. These measures include TLS/SSL encryption for data in transit, AES-256 encryption for data at rest, Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC), and strict Row Level Security (RLS) policies within our database architecture.

Despite our comprehensive security protocols, no internet-based transmission or digital storage system is entirely infallible. We cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a legally defined data breach compromising your personal data, we will notify you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) without undue delay as required by the NDPA.

Data Retention: We retain your account data and extracted workspace data only for as long as your account remains active or as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. Upon account deletion, your proprietary workspace data is permanently scrubbed from our active databases, subject only to temporary retention in encrypted operational backups for disaster recovery purposes (which are cyclically overwritten).

6. Your Data Protection Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) and other applicable global regulations (such as the GDPR and CCPA, where applicable to international users), you possess specific rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You may request a comprehensive copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request corrections to any inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You may request the permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data, subject to limited legal or legitimate business retention exceptions (e.g., tax compliance records).
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You may request a temporary halt to the processing of your data under specific dispute circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request your data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object: You may object to the processing of your data for direct marketing purposes at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at the contact information provided at the end of this document. We will respond to all legitimate requests within the legally mandated timeframe (typically 30 days).

7. International Data Transfers

Akosil operates global cloud infrastructure. Consequently, your personal data may be transferred to, stored, and processed in jurisdictions outside of Nigeria. When we engage in cross-border data transfers, we ensure that such transfers comply with the NDPA, utilizing mechanisms such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), adequacy decisions, or explicit user consent, ensuring your data receives a substantially similar degree of protection as it would domestically.

8. Children's Privacy

The Services are intended strictly for general business and professional audiences and are not directed at children under the age of 18 (or the age of legal majority in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we become aware that we have inadvertently gathered data from a child, we will take immediate measures to purge that information from our systems.

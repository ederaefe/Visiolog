1. Introduction and Scope

Welcome to Visiolog ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are strongly committed to keeping secure any information we obtain from you or about you. This Privacy Policy describes our practices with respect to data handled when you access Visiolog, utilize our document extraction and Optical Character Recognition (OCR) services, run self-hosted air-gapped instances, interact with optional third-party integrations (such as Google Sheets), or otherwise use our platform (collectively, the "Services").

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, including but not limited to the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR). By using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the data handling practices described in this document.

2. Information We Collect & Privacy Architecture

Visiolog is designed with a privacy-first, local-first architecture. In self-hosted or local standalone mode, your documents and spreadsheets remain 100% on your local workstation without external network transmissions.

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Data: When registering for an online account, we collect your email address, full name, and authentication credentials. If you utilize single sign-on (SSO) providers like Google, we receive basic profile information permitted by your configuration. In local standalone mode, no online account is required.
  • Billing Information: If you subscribe to a hosted tier, payment processing is handled securely by our third-party payment processor (Paystack/Flutterwave). We do not process, store, or transmit full credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Customer Support Data: Information you provide when contacting us for technical support, including diagnostic information and written correspondence.

2.2 User-Generated Content and Document Data

  • Uploaded Documents: When you upload images, PDFs, or other document formats for OCR processing, files are processed temporarily in volatile memory buffers to extract text and tabular matrices.
  • Zero-Storage Architecture: Uploaded documents are processed in volatile memory and permanently discarded after text is converted into spreadsheet rows. We do not permanently store raw document images.
  • Extracted Data: Extracted spreadsheet datasets are persisted in your configured storage backend (browser IndexedDB for local-first mode, or Supabase for cloud-synchronized mode).

2.3 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage and Telemetry: In cloud-hosted instances, standard operational diagnostic logs may be maintained. In self-hosted and air-gapped instances, external telemetry is deactivated by default.
  • Local Storage: We use local storage and IndexedDB on your device to maintain session state, offline project caches, and user theme preferences.

3. How We Use Your Information

We only process your data when we have a lawful basis to do so under the NDPA and applicable global regulations. We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Service Provision: To authenticate users, provide OCR capabilities, maintain workspace state, and facilitate exports to local formats or external integrations.
  • Platform Improvement: To monitor system reliability and optimize tabular extraction algorithms. We do not use your private document data to train public machine learning models without explicit consent.
  • Communication: To send critical transactional notices (e.g. security alerts, password resets) and product updates.
  • Security and Fraud Prevention: To protect infrastructure integrity, prevent abuse, and mitigate unauthorized access.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable statutory laws or lawful governmental requests.

4. Third-Party Integrations and Data Sharing

4.1 Google Workspace and Google Sheets Integration

Visiolog offers an optional integration with Google Sheets, allowing users to export and synchronize extracted tabular datasets directly to Google Drive upon authorization via OAuth 2.0.

  • Scope of Access: We request only the narrowest permissions required to create new spreadsheets and write row data to the spreadsheets you explicitly select.
  • Data Handling Requirements: Visiolog's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Token Storage: OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest and never used to access unauthorized Google Drive files.

4.2 Service Providers

When utilizing hosted cloud modes, trusted providers may facilitate delivery (e.g. Supabase, Vercel, Paystack). Self-hosted deployments bypass third-party cloud intermediaries entirely.

5. Data Security and Retention

We implement robust security measures including TLS encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, and strict access controls. In local-first mode, all data resides entirely in your client environment.

Data Retention: We retain account and cloud workspace data only while your account remains active or as needed for operational purposes. Deleting your workspace scrubs the corresponding records permanently.
